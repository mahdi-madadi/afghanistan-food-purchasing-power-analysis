## LinkedIn post

I have completed a new data-analysis portfolio project on food prices and wage purchasing power in Afghanistan.

Using World Food Programme market data, I analyzed 34 monitored markets from May 2020 to December 2025. I calculated how many kilograms of low-quality wheat flour one reported paid day of unskilled work could purchase each month.

The analysis found that median flour purchasing power fell from 8.81 kg per paid workday in May 2020 to 5.78 kg in June 2022. It then recovered to 12.57 kg in July 2024 and ended at 10.04 kg in December 2025.

This project strengthened my practical skills in:

- Excel data-quality checks and documentation
- Power BI data modeling, DAX measures, and dashboards
- Python validation and reproducible outputs
- Research writing and presentation for academic and NGO audiences

I also documented the study limitations carefully. The indicator measures the flour value of one reported paid workday. It does not show employment availability, household income, or food security by itself.

The full project includes the raw-data audit, Power BI dashboard, Python validation, research paper, and presentation:

https://github.com/mahdi-madadi/afghanistan-food-purchasing-power-analysis
